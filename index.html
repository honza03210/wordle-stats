How the stats were worked out, and merged nicknames
Avg (X=7)
Average guesses over every game, with a failed game counted as 7. Main ranking number.
Avg solved
Average guesses over solved games only.
Day win %
Share of days (with at least one other player) where the player had the best score, ties included.
Beats field %
On average, the share of that day's other players the player beat (a tie counts half).
Best streak
Most consecutive calendar days solved.
Data cleaning
Lines of “:” in the export are separators. Names after them are Discord nicknames of people whose account wasn't linked; multi-word nicknames were rejoined. Where the bot posted two days under one date, the earlier post was moved back a day.

Nicknames merged into accounts. Most are inferred from never appearing on the same day as the account over dozens of games, so treat them as likely, not certain.
